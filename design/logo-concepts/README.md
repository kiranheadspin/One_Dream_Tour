# One Dream Group logo explorations

These concepts use the supplied One Dream Group mark and the final One Dream Cup UI as visual references. They are intentionally non-destructive: the live header remains unchanged until a direction is selected.

## 01 — Stadium Crest

The strongest bridge from the existing woven identity to the premium corporate-sports system. Interlocked royal-blue ribbons sit inside a gold victory crest. Recommended for the primary website and event identity.

Prompt direction: preserve the interlocking/togetherness DNA; evolve it into a symmetrical woven diamond, shield and upward chevrons; use flat navy, gold, blue and white; render the exact One Dream Group name and tagline; avoid gaming, betting, literal sports equipment and decorative effects.

## 02 — Kinetic Weave

The most energetic option. Three angular ribbons form an abstract ODG signal and upward movement. Best for a younger, high-motion corporate-events brand.

Prompt direction: simplify the original weave into a forward-moving ODG and arrow; prioritize momentum, unity and ambition; use a gold-led palette with one blue ribbon; retain exact name and tagline; avoid generic swooshes and esports styling.

## 03 — Monogram Signal

The most minimal and versatile option. A compact geometric ODG monogram uses negative space and a small royal-blue signal point. Best for favicons, social avatars, merchandise and small product surfaces.

Prompt direction: construct a precise interlocked ODG monogram with a subtle diamond/victory point; make it suitable for embroidery and one-colour printing; use gold, white and a small blue accent; retain exact name and tagline; avoid literal dream imagery and common infinity marks.

## Suggested system

- Primary lockup: Stadium Crest
- Compact app/favicon mark: Monogram Signal
- Campaign or motion identity: Kinetic Weave

All three were generated with the built-in image-generation workflow as polished raster concepts. The selected direction should be rebuilt as clean SVG paths, then exported as transparent SVG/PNG, single-colour, reversed, favicon and social variants before replacing the production header asset.
